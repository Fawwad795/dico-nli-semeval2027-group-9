# Minervini and Riedel 2018: Adversarially regularising NLI models with logical background knowledge

Pasquale Minervini, Sebastian Riedel. *Adversarially Regularising Neural NLI Models to Integrate Logical Background Knowledge.* CoNLL 2018, Brussels. Verified 2026-09-29 at https://aclanthology.org/K18-1007/.

## What it says

The authors generate adversarial sentence pairs on which an NLI model violates first-order constraints such as the symmetry of contradiction and the transitivity of entailment, then train against those violations. The regularised models are more robust on adversarial data and violate the constraints less often.

## Why it matters for DiCo-NLI

It is the earliest of the consistency-regularisation papers for NLI and, with Li et al. 2019, frames reversal consistency as a constraint a model can be trained to respect rather than a property to hope for.
