# Agirre et al. 2016: SemEval-2016 Task 2, Interpretable Semantic Textual Similarity

Eneko Agirre, Aitor Gonzalez-Agirre, Iñigo Lopez-Gazpio, Montse Maritxalar, German Rigau, Larraitz Uria. *SemEval-2016 Task 2: Interpretable Semantic Textual Similarity.* SemEval-2016, San Diego. Verified 2026-09-29 at https://aclanthology.org/S16-1082/.

## What it says

A shared task in which systems do not only score the similarity of two sentences but explain it: sentences are split into chunks, chunks are aligned across the pair, and each alignment gets a relation type and a similarity score.

## Why it matters for DiCo-NLI

The chunk alignments of interpretable STS are where PhrasIS phrases come from, which is why DiCo-NLI items are noun phrases, verb chains and prepositional phrases rather than sentences. It explains the three-word average length and the frequency of prepositional phrases in the data.
