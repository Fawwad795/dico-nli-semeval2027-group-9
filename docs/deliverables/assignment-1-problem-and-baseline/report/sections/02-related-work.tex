\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{The pilot study.}
The task grows out of \citet{apaolaza-larraya-etal-2026-assessing}, who defined the two consistency measures (they call them soft and hard causal coherence) and ran encoder, decoder and encoder-decoder transformers on PhrasIS phrase pairs, each tuned with up to 150 Optuna trials on an 80/20 split of the training data. Encoders led throughout. DeBERTa-v3-base reached weighted F1 0.75, soft coherence 0.84 and hard coherence 0.79 on the combined positives track; decoder-only models trailed on both accuracy and coherence, and every family lost ground on pairs it had not seen. Their manual error analysis points at prepositional phrases and at near-synonym or taxonomic relations. We cite these numbers more than once, but their track has no negatives and a different split, so for us they are a reference point and not a target.

\paragraph{Direction and the reversal curse.}
The framing comes from \citet{berglund-etal-2024-reversal}: an autoregressive language model fine-tuned on ``A is B'' cannot answer ``B is A''. \citet{lv-etal-2024-analysis} trace this to the next-token objective and its causal attention, and repair it at training time. DiCo-NLI is a gentler setting, since both phrases are on the table, yet the pilot's decoder results suggest the asymmetry survives. Direction is fragile elsewhere too. \citet{yanaka-etal-2019-help} found that NLI models handle upward monotonicity inferences far better than downward ones, and at phrase level that is exactly the forward against backward distinction.

\paragraph{Consistency as a constraint.}
\citet{minervini-riedel-2018-adversarially} and \citet{li-etal-2019-logic} turned logical relations among groups of NLI examples, such as the symmetry of contradiction and transitivity, into training penalties, and improved accuracy and consistency together. \citet{elazar-etal-2021-measuring} define consistency as invariance under meaning-preserving rewording and measure it on ParaRel. Reversal belongs to the same family of constraints, except that the label has to change in a prescribed way rather than stay put.

\paragraph{Data and models.}
The phrases are from PhrasIS \citep{lopez-gazpio-etal-2024-phrasis}, which annotates phrase pairs taken from the chunk alignments of interpretable semantic textual similarity \citep{agirre-etal-2016-semeval-2016}. That origin is why the items are noun phrases, verb chains and prepositional phrases rather than sentences. Our encoder is DeBERTa-v3-base \citep{he-etal-2023-debertav3}, the strongest base-size model in the pilot.
