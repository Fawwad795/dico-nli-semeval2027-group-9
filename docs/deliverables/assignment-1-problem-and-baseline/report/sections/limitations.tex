\section*{Limitations}

Everything here is on one track, English, and on the development set, which stands in for the unreleased test set. The classical baselines use one seed and fixed settings; the encoder uses three seeds and a fixed recipe. Nothing was tuned, so these numbers are baselines, not upper bounds. The misleading length-cue slice has 10 instances. The error categories come from 20 hand-picked errors and describe kinds of failure, not their frequency. The part-of-speech and similarity features depend on spaCy's small English model and on one sentence encoder, both run on phrases with no context around them.
