\section{Next Steps}
\label{sec:next}

Assignment 2 moves to decoder language models, where the pilot found the largest consistency gap. We will compare open-weight instruction-tuned models used by prompting and fine-tuned with low-rank adapters, and test three mechanisms for direction: training on both orders of every pair, a loss term that penalises incompatible predictions on a pair and its reversal in the manner of \citet{li-etal-2019-logic}, and prompts that present both orders together. Every system will be reported with weighted F1, macro F1, \SoftCons{}, \HardCons{}, the consistent-but-wrong count and the length-cue slices of \cref{tab:slices}, against the baselines of this report. We will also audit the conflicting and questionable gold labels before relying on the error categories, and add the Spanish and Basque tracks if time allows.
