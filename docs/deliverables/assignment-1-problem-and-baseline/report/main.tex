\begin{abstract}
SemEval-2027 Task 2 (DiCo-NLI) asks for one of four relations between two ordered phrases and also scores whether a system's answers on a pair and on its reversal are compatible. On the English track we ask how much of a fine-tuned encoder's accuracy and consistency surface cues alone recover. A selective exploratory analysis of 3,042 training instances shows direction leaking through form: the premise is the longer phrase in 78\% of forward-entailment instances, and every hypothesis word occurs in the premise in 45\% of them. A logistic regression on 17 such cues reaches weighted F1 0.531 and \SoftCons{} 0.794 on the development set, the most self-consistent of our classical baselines. DeBERTa-v3-base, fine-tuned over three seeds, reaches 0.807 $\pm$ 0.015 weighted F1, 0.877 \SoftCons{} and 0.810 \HardCons{}. Its advantage sits on instances where length carries no signal and on the boundary between equivalence and entailment; its remaining errors involve one-word substitutions, prepositions and numerals. A constant \EQ{} predictor obtains \SoftCons{} 1.0, which is why we read the three official scores only together.
\end{abstract}
